Purge needless shit:

Most North Americans carry around an abundant amount of needless shit. We cram our houses with it, or store it in various storage facilities not to be seen for several years. But do we really need this stuff that we store away? When our stuff begins to be a burden on us, then it is probably time to get rid of it. Purging our needless possessions can be a difficult and time consuming task, but the end result can save us hundreds of dollars and give us an overall sense of freedom.
